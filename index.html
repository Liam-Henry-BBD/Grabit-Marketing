Powerful Features

Everything you need to manage projects effectively and keep your team motivated.

Task Management

Create, assign, and track tasks with ease. Set priorities, deadlines, and point values based on task difficulty.

Gamified Workflow

Turn work into a game with points, leaderboards, and competitive nature. Keep your team engaged and motivated.

Task Grabbing

Team members can "grab" available tasks, moving them from the available pool to their personal workspace.

Point System

Assign point values to tasks based on difficulty: Simple (5 points), Medium (10 points), and Hard (15 points).

Leaderboards

Track individual and team performance with leaderboards showing accumulated points and completed tasks.

Google Authentication

Secure login using Google authentication, making it easy for development teams to get started.

How GrabIt Works

A simple workflow that transforms how your team manages projects

1

Create a Project

The team lead creates a project and sets up tasks with descriptions and deadlines

Assign Points

2

Tasks are assigned point values based on difficulty (5, 10, or 15 points).

3

Team Grabs Tasks

Team memebers browse available tasks and "grab" ones they want to work on. You can even add collaborators on tasks

Track Progress

4

Tasks move through statuses: Available, Grabbed, Reviewed, and Complete.

Dashboards

Project Overview Dashboard
